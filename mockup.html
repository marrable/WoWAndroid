Try the targeting

  1. Tap the game on the wolf, the ally or a party frame to target it, as a mouse click would.
  2. Hold Enemy and drag to aim the red circle, then lift to let go. One thumb does what takes RB plus the right stick on a pad.
  3. Tap Enemy or Friend quickly to keep the mode on. Its buttons replace the action grid and Look becomes Aim. Tap it again, or pick a target, to go back.
  4. Friend mode adds Party 1 to 4, one tap per party member, for healing.

Controls view

  • Twelve one-tap action buttons; Shift keeps a second set of twelve on until tapped again. No held layers.
  • Forever's own targeting modes are used as they are: Enemy holds RB, Friend holds LB, and the mode buttons are the face buttons and D-pad the game shows for them.
  • Jump, Clear, Use and the mode buttons go out as gamepad input; the action buttons as keys, unless keys turn the gamepad interface off.

Keyboard view

  • Opening it opens the chat box; Enter sends, Esc closes.
  • Keys split between the two thumbs, channel and emote buttons in the middle.
  • The pane hides when a physical controller connects.
